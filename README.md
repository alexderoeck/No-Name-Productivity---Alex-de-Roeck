### Overview
  
**My college coursework for my Level 3 Computing BTEC:** [No Name Productivity](https://x.thunkable.com/projectPage/69b9b8e93b41191d069e1c39), a Pomodoro-style study timer app built in Thunkable to support students with their study skills and independence.
  
### The Idea
A Pomodoro timer splits work into short bursts of focused study followed by breaks, helping users concentrate for manageable periods rather than burning out. The app manages this cycle automatically so students can focus on their actual work, rather than on managing their own time.

### Features
- **Focus, Break, and Long Break timers** — fully adjustable durations and break intervals via a dedicated Modify screen (sliders with sensible min/max ranges, e.g. 15–60 min focus sessions), so the app can be tailored to how each student likes to work.
- **Pause/Resume and Reset** — with buttons that disable/enable at the right moments to prevent misuse (e.g. can't modify settings mid-timer without pausing first).
- **Skip Session** — with a confirmation pop-up, added after a beta tester accidentally skipped and lost their streak.
- **Gamification** — a focus streak that increments each completed session and resets on a skip, plus an all-time streak highscore saved to the device, and short on-screen motivational messages during focus and break sessions.
- **Colour-changing UI** — shades of red during focus sessions (energy, urgency) and blue during breaks (calm, tranquillity), based on colour psychology, using the "Fiery Ocean" Coolors palette.
- **Accessibility** — high-contrast text on coloured backgrounds, large readable fonts (Oswald/Poppins), and predictable, labelled UI elements so the app needs no additional guidance to use.

### Process
Designed with Balsamiq wireframes, refined after two rounds of peer feedback (e.g. making buttons more clearly clickable), then built and iteratively debugged in Thunkable — including replacing buggy "Forever" loops with Thunkable's Timer component to fix crashing and freezing issues. The finished app was tested against a written functionality/usability/stability test plan, passed all functionality tests, then went through a second round of beta feedback that led to a layout adjustment (controls moved lower for one-handed use), the skip confirmation, the streak highscore, and device vibration when a timer ends. A later stress test revealed a soft-lock bug, noted as a future fix.
